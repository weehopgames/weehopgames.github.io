# Hood Hop Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 27 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Android game **Hood Hop** ("the game"), made
by **Wee Hop Games** ("we", "us"), handles and why. If you have questions, contact us at
the email address at the end of this page.

## Summary

- We don't ask for your name, email, account or any other personal details.
- The game saves only your high score, and only on your device.
- The game shows ads from **Google AdMob**. Google collects some device data to show
  and measure those ads, as described below.
- In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, you're asked for consent first, and you can change
  your choice at any time from the game's home screen.

## Information the game stores

The game saves your **high score** on your device so it's still there next time you
play. It is never sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data
deletes it.

We don't run our own servers, accounts, analytics or crash reporting, so we receive no
information about you from the game.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows a banner ad and, if you choose to watch one, rewarded video ads, served by
Google AdMob. To serve, personalise (where allowed) and measure ads, and to prevent
fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the game automatically collects:

- **Device identifiers**: your device's advertising ID and app set ID.
- **IP address**, which may be used to estimate your general location.
- **Product interactions**: for example app launches, taps and ad views.
- **Diagnostic information**: for example app performance and crash data related to ads.

This data is sent to Google, encrypted in transit. Google's use of it is covered by
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and
[How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
We don't receive this data in a form that identifies you.

**Your choices**

- **Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland):** on first launch the game shows Google's consent
  form, where you can accept, refuse or choose which uses and partners to allow. You can
  change your choice any time with the **privacy options button** (the shield icon) on
  the game's home screen. If you refuse, you may still see ads, but they won't be
  personalised (they may be non-personalised or limited ads).
- **Advertising ID:** you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of
  personalised ads, in your device's settings (on most devices: Settings › Privacy › Ads,
  or Settings › Google › Ads). See
  [Google Play Help: Advertising ID](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/3405269).

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal
information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through
the game, contact us and we'll help have it removed.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA and UK), you may have
the right to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its use. We hold no
personal data about you ourselves; for data collected by Google for ads, use the choices
above or contact Google through its privacy policy. You can also contact us and we'll help.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
